    About this map

    Each dot is one person at one home, shown for the years the records place them there: from the first record (birth, marriage, a dated address, a child born in the house) to their death or the first record somewhere else.

    The people, families and homes come from the Ortsfamilienbuch Roseln bis 1925 (Albrich), which is compiled from the Roseln church registers. The stories in “Life & records” also draw on Christian Weiss, Roseln mitten in Siebenbürgen (2010): about 700 facts from across the book – offices, gifts to the church, quarrels, the 1719 plague, both world wars, the 1945 deportation – each matched to the person it names and cited by page. Where the book could mean more than one person, the fact was left out. Parents are linked through the book's own family numbers. Houses are placed from the 1869 house numbers, read off the 1869 village plan in Weiss, Roseln mitten in Siebenbürgen (2010), p.455. People without a house number are shown at the church.
